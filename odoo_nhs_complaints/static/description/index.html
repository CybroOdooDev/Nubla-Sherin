NHS Complaints & PALS Management

v19.0.1.0.0 OPL-1

Complete statutory complaints handling and PALS concern management for NHS organisations. Part of the NHS Quality Suite alongside NHS Incident & Risk Management — complaints, incidents, risks and corrective actions in one integrated system.

Why this module? Unlike standalone complaints tools (Datix Complaints, iCasework), a complaint here sits in the same system as the incident it may reveal and the risk it evidences — one click from complaint to incident to risk to action.

What's included

PALS Concern Management

Lightweight informal path aiming at same-day / 48-hour resolution. One-click escalation into a formal complaint without re-keying.

Full Statutory Complaint Lifecycle

Capture → 3-working-day acknowledgement → investigation → CEO-signed response → closure, with re-opened and PHSO branches.

Complainant & Consent Handling

Third-party representatives, advocates, MPs, solicitors, deceased-patient next-of-kin. Consent gate blocks response send until obtained.

KO41a Annual Return

Built-in KO41a-aligned subject taxonomy. Export wizard generates the annual return for NHS Digital submission with one click.

PHSO Escalation Tracking

Track referral, PHSO case reference, outcome, recommendations and resulting actions. Board-visible PHSO casebook dashboard.

Incident & Risk Integration

Two-way link to nhs.incident and nhs.risk. Create or link an incident from a complaint; see linked complaints on every incident.
