NHS Incident & Risk Management

Datix-class incident reporting & risk register for every CQC-registered provider

Key Features

  • Public web form (no login) modelled on the Datix DIF1 — unlimited reporters
  • PSIRF-aligned investigation workflow: SWARM, AAR, MDT Review, PSII
  • Duty of Candour tracker with 10-working-day clock and letter generation
  • RIDDOR determination wizard with full audit log
  • CQC statutory notification tracker
  • LFPSE-aligned event taxonomy and CSV/JSON export
  • 5×5 NPSA risk register with tiered registers (local → BAF)
  • CAPA action management with overdue escalation
  • Provider-type adaptation: NHS Trust, GP Practice, Care Home, Hospice & more

Regulatory Compliance Built In

This module encodes four statutory frameworks as first-class engines: PSIRF, LFPSE, Duty of Candour (CQC Reg 20), and RIDDOR 2013.

Built for the NHS. Ready for every CQC-registered provider — care homes, GP practices, hospices, independent hospitals, pharmacies and dental practices.
