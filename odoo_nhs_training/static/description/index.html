Frequently Asked Questions

How is a workforce member's required training determined?

The member's post drives its Requirement Profile, and/or their staff group carries its own requirements. On top of that, individual-level overrides can add an extra subject or waive one for that specific person, with a reason and review date — the combined result is the member's required subject set shown on the Required & Status tab and in the Training Matrix.

What happens if a member fails an assessment?

Setting the training record's Result to Fail forces its status to Failed, regardless of expiry date, and the member is treated as non-compliant for that subject — exactly like an expired record — until a later completion supersedes it with a Pass. The failed attempt is kept as history, never deleted, so the evidence trail stays intact.

Can the module generate a certificate of completion?

Yes. Clicking Print Certificate on any training record generates a branded PDF Certificate of Completion directly from that record's own data — member, subject, completion date, provider, validity and certificate reference — with no external file to source or upload.
