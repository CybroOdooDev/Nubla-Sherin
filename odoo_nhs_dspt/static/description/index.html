Key Highlights

Versioned NHS England Editions

Each DSPT year is its own edition, pre-loaded with the ten National Data Guardian standards and their assertions — clone last year's edition into the new one in a single click.

Standard → Assertion → Evidence

Drill straight from a standard down into its assertions and the named, mandatory-flagged evidence items behind each one — with built-in guidance on exactly what to upload.

Gap-to-Action Workflow

Marking an evidence item Not Met raises a pre-filled Improvement Action with an owner, priority and target date, so every compliance gap becomes tracked, verifiable work.

Stale Evidence Tracking

Evidence review dates are checked against a configurable staleness threshold, so proof that's gone out of date is flagged even while its assertion still shows Met.

Live Readiness Dashboard & Reports

A deadline countdown, readiness % by standard and owner completion at a glance, plus one-click Status Report, Improvement Plan and Assurance Summary PDFs.

DSPT User Access Levels

Use the DSPT Access Level field to assign the appropriate role to each user. The selected access level determines the user's permissions within the NHS DSPT Compliance module.

Every DSPT Year, One Place

The DSPT Editions list shows every toolkit year side by side with Publication Deadline, Assertion, Evidence and Assessment counts and an Active status badge — so you always know which NHS England edition is live and how big it is.

Editions Built From the Ten National Data Guardian Standards

Each DSPT edition includes the ten National Data Guardian standards. Every standard is pre-configured with its related assertions, making it easy to create and manage assessments for each toolkit year.

Drill Down From Standard to Assertion

Every line on the Standards tab carries a View link, taking you straight from a standard down into its assertion — the framework is fully clickable, not a flat spreadsheet.

Standards Carry Their Own Assertions

Opening the "Personal Confidential Data" standard reveals its Assertions tab with each assertion's reference, text and evidence count — the hierarchy from standard to assertion is explicit and navigable.

One Click to the Assertion Text

Every assertion behind a standard is one click away for review or editing — no hunting through a spreadsheet to find the wording that matters.

Assertions Break Down Into Named Evidence Items

Each assertion expands into its Evidence Items tab — a DPO in place, a maintained ROPA, completed DPIAs and more — every item flagged Mandatory or not, with an Evidence Type Hint so nobody has to guess what proof is actually required.

Mandatory Evidence, Flagged Up Front

Every evidence line's mandatory flag and type hint — such as "Named role / appointment letter" — is visible before you even open the record, cutting down on ambiguity during the self-assessment.

DetailedEvidence Guidance

Every evidence item includes detailed guidance, a mandatory indicator, applicable organisation types, and an evidence type hint to help users upload the correct supporting documents.

Edition Notes

The Notes tab lets administrators record important information, guidance, or changes related to a DSPT edition for future reference.

Clone Last Year's Edition Into the New One

The "Clone to New Year" wizard deep-clones every standard, assertion and evidence item into a new edition and flags each copied item "New" — building next year's DSPT takes one click instead of a rebuild from scratch.

New Editions Start in Draft, Fully Populated

A freshly cloned edition opens in Draft with every standard and its assertion counts already copied across, plus Set Active / Archive / Clone to New Year actions and a chatter log recording the creation event.

Manage DSPT Assessments

View and manage all DSPT assessments in one place. Track readiness, gaps, deadlines, achievement status, and assessment progress, with quick access to Current Assessment, My Items, and Gaps & Actions.

Comprehensive DSPT Assessments

Create and manage DSPT assessments with readiness tracking, achievement status, deadlines, assertions, evidence, gaps, actions, and notes—all from a single assessment screen.

Generate the Assessment From the Edition's Framework

The "Generate Assessment Lines" wizard creates assertion and evidence lines filtered to the items applicable to your organisation type, and is safe to re-run whenever the edition gains new items.

Track Every Assertion's Status

The assessment form shows its full workflow statusbar — Draft / In Progress / Ready / Published / Submitted — live Readiness % and Achieved Status stats, and an Assertions tab listing every assertion with Mandatory Met/Total counts and a per-row status badge.

Assertion-Level Status, Rolled Up From Evidence

Each assertion has its own Not Started / In Progress / Met / Not Met statusbar and Mandatory Met vs Mandatory Total counters, computed directly from its underlying evidence items, owners, statuses and stale flags.

Mandatory Evidence, Flagged With a Ribbon

A mandatory evidence item carries a diagonal "MANDATORY" ribbon plus Start Work / Mark N/A buttons, a Guidance block, and Answer/Evidence sections with an Evidence Reference/Location field — everything needed to work the item lives on one screen.

Move Evidence Through Its Lifecycle

Mark Met / Mark Not Met / Mark N/A action buttons drive a deliberate, auditable path from Not Started to a final determination — rather than a free-text field anyone can edit without a trace.

Turn a "Not Met" Into an Improvement Action

Marking evidence Not Met surfaces a "Raise Improvement Action" button that opens a pre-filled action with Owner, Priority, Target Date and an Open / In Progress / Completed / Verified statusbar — gaps become tracked work in one click.

Close the Loop With Verified Evidence

A completed improvement action shows its Verified state, a completion note and an attached file as proof — giving auditors a documented trail from gap to resolution.

Recompute Readiness On Demand

Clicking Recompute on the assessment instantly re-scores readiness %, gap count, stale count and achieved status, updating the evidence list live — no manual percentage math, ever.

See Every Compliance Gap at a Glance

The assessment's Gaps tab lists every outstanding item with its Standard, Owner and how many Actions have been raised against it — the single source of truth for what's still missing.

Every Action, Owned and Dated

The Actions tab shows every raised improvement action with its Owner, a star-based Priority, a Target Date and its current status — turning the improvement plan into a real task list rather than a wishlist.

Submit and Lock the Record

Once submitted, the assessment displays a red diagonal "LOCKED" ribbon, a Submission Reference, Submission Date and Published By/At fields, with a full chatter history of every state transition — a tamper-evident record for audit.

Print Straight From the Assessment

The assessment's Print menu exposes three one-click reports — DSPT Status Report, DSPT Improvement Plan and DSPT Assurance Summary — right alongside Duplicate and Delete, so reporting is never a separate export step.

A Status Report NHS England Would Recognise

The DSPT Status Report PDF opens with headline stats — Readiness %, Achieved Status, Gaps Outstanding and Deadline — then a full Readiness-by-Standard table and an Outstanding Mandatory Items table listing exactly what's missing and who owns it.

Jump Straight to This Year's Work

The "Current Assessment" menu filters to assessments for the active edition that aren't yet submitted, showing Readiness %, Gaps, Deadline and both Achieved Status and workflow Status badges side by side for quick triage.

My Items: What's On You

The "My Items" view groups a logged-in owner's assigned evidence by status, so every owner gets a personal to-do list of outstanding mandatory items instead of a 50-line spreadsheet to search.

Gaps & Actions, Company-Wide

The global Gaps & Actions list rolls up improvement actions across every assessment and edition, each with Owner, Priority, Target Date and Status — one screen to see whether every raised gap is actually being closed.

The Full Evidence Library

The Evidence Library lists every evidence line across every assessment with Reference, Standard, Mandatory flag, Owner, Status, Evidence Review date and a Stale/Expired indicator — overdue or Not Met items are picked out in red so risk is visible at a glance.

Bulk Assign Owner for Evidence Items

The Bulk Assign Owner option lets you assign one owner to multiple selected evidence records at the same time.

Bulk Assign Owner Wizard

Using this wizard, you can assign a new owner to all selected evidence records at once.

Catch Evidence Before It Goes Stale

The Stale Evidence view, grouped by Standard, surfaces evidence whose review date has aged past the configured threshold — even when it's still marked Met — because proof doesn't stay valid forever.

A Live Dashboard for Readiness at a Glance

The DSPT Readiness Dashboard opens with a deadline countdown banner, KPI tiles for Readiness %, Gaps, Overdue Actions and Stale Evidence, a colour-coded Readiness-by-Standard bar list, and an Owner Completion panel showing exactly who has outstanding items.

Print the Status Report Anytime

The same DSPT Status Report is reachable straight from the Reporting menu, generating the identical Readiness-by-Standard breakdown and Outstanding Mandatory Items table on demand — not just at submission time.

An Improvement Plan You Can Hand to Auditors

The Improvement Plan PDF lists every raised action with its Reference, Gap, Owner, Priority, Target Date and Status — a ready-made document for governance meetings or supplier assurance requests.

A One-Page Assurance Certificate for Partners

The DSPT Assurance Summary renders as a formal certificate-style PDF with a large Achieved Status badge, Readiness %, Submission date and Reference, with a clear disclaimer that the NHS DSPT portal remains the authoritative record — making it safe to share with suppliers and partners.

Model Every Organisation Type

Organisation Profiles configuration lists NHS Trust, GP Practice, Care Provider, Supplier/Partner and Other, each with a short code used to filter which assertions and evidence items actually apply to your organisation.

Tune Deadlines and Staleness Thresholds

The NHS DSPT settings panel exposes Deadline Reminder Lead Time, Stale Evidence Age and Approaching Standards Threshold — letting managers control exactly when reminders fire and when evidence counts as stale.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is an assessment's readiness % calculated?

Each evidence item's status — Not Started, In Progress, Met, Not Met or N/A — rolls up into its assertion, and every assertion rolls up into the assessment's overall Readiness % and Achieved Status. Clicking Recompute re-scores the whole assessment instantly, so the number on screen always reflects the latest evidence and action changes.

What happens when an evidence item is marked Not Met?

Marking evidence Not Met surfaces a "Raise Improvement Action" button that opens a pre-filled action for that gap, with an owner, priority and target date already set. The action moves through Open → In Progress → Completed → Verified, and once verified the evidence can be recomputed to Met — the gap never just disappears without a documented resolution.

Can the module generate reports for submission and audit?

Yes. Every assessment's Print menu generates a DSPT Status Report, DSPT Improvement Plan and DSPT Assurance Summary directly from that record's own data — readiness by standard, outstanding mandatory items, raised actions and submission reference — with no external file to source or upload.
