Frequently Asked Questions

Who can access governance records?

Access is controlled by a per-user Governance Access Level — Governance Viewer, Committee Secretary / Governance Officer, or Governance Manager / Company Secretary — so only the right people can view or edit committees, meetings and declarations.

What happens if a committee can't make quorum?

The committee record automatically shows a warning when the minimum number of voting members or non-executive directors required for quorum is not met by the current membership, so it's visible before a meeting is even scheduled.

How are conflicts of interest managed during a meeting?

Each declaration logged at a meeting is linked to the relevant agenda item and tracked through to a conflict-management outcome — noted, withdrawn from the item, left the room, or no action required.
