Frequently Asked Questions

What is backlog maintenance, and how is it recorded in this module?

Backlog maintenance refers to the estimated cost of bringing buildings and spaces up to an acceptable standard. It represents the work that needs to be done but hasn't been completed yet.

In this module, you can record backlog items against any building or space with:

  • Cost estimate – The expected rectification cost
  • Risk category – High / Significant / Moderate / Low (the exact categories required for ERIC reporting)
  • Element type – Roof, structure, fabric, mechanical, electrical, fire, external, or other
  • Target year – When you plan to fix it
  • Status – Identified → Planned → In Progress → Resolved

The module automatically rolls up backlog costs to the building and site levels, giving you instant visibility of total liabilities.

How does the condition survey workflow work?

The condition survey workflow follows the NHS Six Facet methodology:

Step 1: A surveyor assesses a building or space and scores each of the six facets on the A–D scale:

Grade Meaning
A As new / excellent
B Sound / operationally safe
C Operational but needs major repair soon
D Unacceptable / serious risk

Step 2: The six facets scored are: Physical condition, Statutory compliance, Functional suitability, Space utilisation, Quality/environment, and Energy performance.

Step 3: The module automatically calculates the overall grade (default: worst-of-all-facets) and updates the building's latest condition grade.

Step 4: The survey history is preserved, allowing you to track condition trends over time and plan investment accordingly.

How do areas and measurements roll up through the hierarchy?

The module uses a bottom-up roll-up system for area measurements:

The flow:
Space → Floor → Building → Site

Example: If you have a building with three floors, each with 500m² of space, the building total GIA is 1,500m², and the site total GIA is the sum of all its buildings.

Other measurements tracked:

  • NIA (Net Internal Area) – Usable area excluding circulation and plant
  • Land area – In hectares at the site level
  • Clinical vs non-clinical split – Automatically derived from space functions

All areas are stored in m² (land in hectares), with no unit mixing.
