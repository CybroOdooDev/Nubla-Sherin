Job Planning Access Levels

Assign a Job Planning Access Level — No, Doctor, Clinical Manager or Medical Director/Admin — to each user directly from Settings, alongside their access to the other NHS suite modules.

My Plan

Doctors see their own job plans at a glance — reference, plan year, contracted PAs, total PAs, PA balance and DCC:SPA ratio — scoped to just their own record.

Signed & Locked Job Plan

Once agreed and countersigned by both doctor and manager, a job plan is stamped Signed and locked, with contracted PAs, FTE, on-call profile and the weekly timetable all summarised on one page.

Weekly Timetable Activity

Add a timetable line with day, week pattern, start/end time and a premium-time flag, then classify it via a reusable Session Category into Direct Clinical Care, Supporting Professional Activities, Additional Responsibility or External Duty.

On-Call Commitment

Link a configurable On-Call Profile to the plan to capture this doctor's rota frequency and category, automatically rolling predictable and unpredictable on-call PAs into the plan's Additional PAs total.

Personal Objectives

Record personal objectives with a target date, status (Not Started, On Track, At Risk, Achieved, Not Achieved), description and review notes — each one optionally linked to a wider trust Service Objective.

Two-Party Sign-Off

Both doctor and clinical manager sign independently, each timestamped and attributed, with an Escalation/Mediation Note and Supporting Resources field for when agreement doesn't come easily.

Team Job Plans

Clinical managers see every job plan across their directorate — grouped by unit, with contracted PAs, total PAs, PA balance and DCC:SPA ratio compared side by side.

Objectives Across the Team

Browse every doctor's objectives in one place, grouped by doctor, plan year, status, target date or the Service Objective they support.

Objective Detail View

Open any objective directly to review its target date, linked service objective, full description and review notes alongside its progress status.

Job Plan Gaps

The Gaps view lists every medical post without a current signed job plan, so managers can chase the ones still stuck in Draft before the review deadline.

Annual Year Rollover

Roll a doctor's signed plan forward into the new plan year in one action, optionally scoped to specific directorates and limited to only signed plans.

Bulk Reminders

Send review-due or plans-due reminders in bulk to every doctor and manager with an outstanding job plan for the selected plan year.

Capacity & Completeness Dashboard

A real-time dashboard tracks completeness, gaps, unsigned plans and stalled discussions for the current plan year, with a directorate-by-directorate completeness breakdown.

Job Plan Analysis

Pivot and analyse DCC, SPA, Additional and External PAs, PA balance, FTE and revision number across every job plan, grouped by status and directorate.

Session Categories

Maintain a reusable library of session categories — Outpatient Clinic, Theatre List, Ward Round, MDT, CPD/Study and more — each with a default DCC/SPA/Additional/External classification.

On-Call Profiles

Build reusable on-call profiles combining a rota frequency and category with predictable and unpredictable PA values, ready to attach to any doctor's plan.

On-Call Profile Detail

Each on-call profile shows its supplement rate, category, availability supplement %, and the predictable/unpredictable PAs that make up its total on-call PA contribution.

On-Call Supplement Table

Configure the on-call supplement table — frequency code, rota frequency, category and availability supplement % — that every On-Call Profile draws from.

Supplement Rate Detail

Each supplement rate record pairs a frequency (1 in N) and category with its availability supplement percentage, kept active or archived as trust policy changes.

Plan Years

Every job-planning year is listed with its date range, job plan count, completeness % and status — Draft, Open or Closed.

Plan Year Lifecycle

Open a plan year to accept job plans, track its completeness live, then Close it or Roll Over its signed plans into the next year when the cycle ends.

Service Objectives

Maintain the trust or directorate-level service objectives — like 18-Week RTT Compliance or GIRFT Theatre Productivity — that individual doctors' personal objectives can link back to.

Job Planning Settings

Configure PAs per WTE, PA length, the evening premium-time threshold, annual review lead days, stale-discussion threshold and automatic annual rollover — all from one settings page.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Programmed Activity (PA) and how is it split?

A Programmed Activity is a nominal 4-hour session (configurable via PA Length in Settings). Every timetable line is classified as Direct Clinical Care (DCC), Supporting Professional Activities (SPA), Additional Responsibility or External Duty, and rolled up into the plan's DCC:SPA ratio and PA balance against contracted PAs.

How does the two-party sign-off workflow work?

A job plan moves through Draft, Proposed, In Discussion and Agreed before both the doctor and their clinical manager sign it independently. Once both signatures are recorded, the plan is stamped Signed and locked, with an Escalation/Mediation Note available if agreement isn't reached.

How is on-call work captured on a job plan?

Each plan can link to an On-Call Profile — combining a rota frequency and category from the configurable On-Call Supplement Table with predictable and unpredictable on-call PAs — which is automatically added into the plan's Additional PAs total.
