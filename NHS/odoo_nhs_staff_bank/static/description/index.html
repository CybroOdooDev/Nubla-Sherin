NHS Staff Bank Management

The internal flexible-staffing bank — bank members, open shifts, offer & booking, availability, rates, the compliance gate, and bank-vs-agency spend.

NHS Staff Bank Management runs an organisation's internal flexible-staffing bank — the pool of workers used to fill temporary gaps instead of paying agency rates. It holds bank members and their skills, publishes open shifts, offers and books them, tracks availability and rates, and — critically — only lets compliant workers be booked. It measures bank-versus-agency fill and spend, the number every NHS board watches.

Key Features

  • Bank-member register — roles, bands, skills, cleared areas
  • Availability management with blackout dates
  • Open-shift register by area, date, band and role
  • Shift offer & booking — broadcast or targeted, accept/decline
  • The compliance gate — reads Mandatory Training where installed, degrades gracefully when it is not
  • Effective-dated rate cards by band, role and shift type
  • Bank-vs-agency tracking and cost-avoidance reporting
  • Worked-shift confirmation and pay computation
  • Fill-rate and bank-vs-agency KPI dashboard
  • Bank-member self-service portal

Part of the Cybrosys NHS Workforce Suite

Depends on the Establishment Register (shifts are cover against established posts/areas) and soft-links to the Mandatory Training module for its compliance gate. Built as the foundation for the e-Rostering capstone, which pushes unfilled roster shifts into this bank.
