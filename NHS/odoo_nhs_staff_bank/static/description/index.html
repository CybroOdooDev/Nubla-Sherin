Key Highlights

Open Shift → Offer → Booking Workflow

Raise a shift needing cover, offer it to the bank — singly, in bulk or as a recurring pattern — and watch it move through Draft, Open, Partially Filled and Filled automatically.

Eligibility-Checked Shift Offers

Member Self-Service Portal

Frequently Asked Questions

How is a member's eligibility for a shift offer decided?

When a shift is offered, each member is checked against the shift's required band, role and skills, their cleared areas and their declared availability. Anyone who doesn't match is shown as ineligible with the exact reason — missing skill, band mismatch, or not available for those dates/times — before the offer is sent, so managers know exactly why someone was skipped.

What happens if a booking would breach a member's safe working-hours limit?

It depends on the Gate Policy set in Configuration ▸ Settings. Hard Block prevents the booking outright while a non-compliant or over-hours situation exists; Warn Only lets the booking go ahead but flags a visible working-time breach warning on the booking record, so the decision and its risk stay on file.

Can the module show how much bank use is saving against agency?

Yes. Escalating an unfilled shift to agency records the agency's cost against the shift, and the Fill-Rate Dashboard compares that to the bank cost using a configurable agency comparator uplift to show cost avoidance. The Fill-Rate & Agency-Displacement and Bank-Spend PDF reports turn the same figures into a document for finance and workforce leads.
