Key Highlights

Demand-Based Rota Generation

Generates duty slots automatically from each unit's effective demand template, matching required headcount, band, role and skills to every shift, date and weekday across the roster period.

Rotation Template Rollout

Rolls a repeating multi-week shift pattern across a roster period for chosen staff in a single action, skipping fixed bank holidays and honouring each rule check as it goes.

Automatic Rules Engine

Checks every duty assignment against ten configurable hard and soft rules — 48-hour average week, 11-hour rest, skill mix, training compliance — blocking unsafe shifts before they are saved.

Gap Escalation to Bank & Agency

Pushes unfilled duties to the Staff Bank as open shifts within each unit's escalation lead time, tracking offers through to bank-filled or on to agency cover with cost recorded.

Leave Capacity Protection

Blocks leave approval once too many of a unit's team would be off on the same day, with an audited Force Approve override reserved for Workforce Admins with a logged reason.

Interactive Roster Grid

A staff-by-day grid for click-to-assign scheduling, showing live fill position, rule violations and shift coverage per day, with rules re-checked against the engine on every change.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does gap escalation to the Staff Bank and agency work?

When a duty stays unfilled inside its unit's escalation lead time, NHS e-Rostering raises a Roster Escalation record and, if auto-escalation is on, pushes it to the Staff Bank as an open shift automatically. The escalation is tracked through Needed, Pushed to Bank, Offered and Bank-Filled; if the bank can't cover it, it can be sent to agency with the agency name and cost recorded, and a scheduled sync pulls bank-shift status back onto the roster.

How does the rules/violations engine keep rosters safe?

Every duty assignment is checked against ten configurable rules covering the 48-hour average week, 11-hour daily rest, weekly rest, consecutive days/nights, skill mix, double-booking, leave conflicts and the training compliance gate. Hard rules block the assignment outright at the point of save; soft rules (like contracted-hours position) allow the save but open a logged violation a manager can resolve or justify with a reason.

Does it integrate with the Staff Bank module, and how does leave capacity work?

Yes — when the Staff Bank module is installed, escalations create real open shifts there and bank hours/bookings feed straight back into the 48-hour week and double-booking rule checks; without it, gaps simply track as unfilled or manually covered. Leave requests are checked against each unit's configured leave-capacity percentage before approval, blocking approval once too many staff would be off on the same day unless a Workforce Admin logs a reason and force-approves the exception.
