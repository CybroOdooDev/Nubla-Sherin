NHS e-Rostering
================
Safe, rule-checked staff rostering on the funded establishment — shift types and rotation
templates, demand-based rotas, a rule-checked roster grid, the training/registration
compliance gate, leave handling, staff self-service, duty swaps, publication, and automatic
escalation of unfilled duties to the Staff Bank (and on to agency) with cost visibility.
