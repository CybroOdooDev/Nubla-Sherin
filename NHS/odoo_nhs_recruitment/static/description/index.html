Key Highlights

Funded Post to Hire, One Pipeline

Every vacancy is raised against a funded post from the Establishment Register — approving it checks capacity up front, and hiring closes the loop by incrementing the post's in-post FTE.

Values-Based Shortlisting & Scoring

Applications and interviews are scored against the vacancy's own weighted Person Specification criteria — structured, auditable, and aligned to the NHS Constitution values.

The Six NHS Employment Check Standards

Identity, right to work, registration, references, DBS and occupational health generate automatically from the offer's Check Profile, and gate the start date until they clear.

Equality Data, Legally Segregated

Equality & diversity monitoring is collected on a separate, access-restricted model — never shown to the selection panel, reported only in anonymised aggregate.

Retention & Anonymisation, Built In

Unsuccessful applicants are automatically anonymised after a configurable retention period, unless they've consented to stay on file in the talent pool.

A Dedicated Recruitment Access Level

Every user gets a Recruitment Access Level — Viewer, Officer, Pre-Employment Checks or Manager — right alongside their other NHS-suite roles, so recruitment permissions are assigned the same way as everything else.

Every Vacancy, Grouped by Stage

The Vacancies kanban groups every funded post being recruited against by status — Draft, Submitted, In Progress, Open — with department, hiring manager, FTE and a live applications count on every card.

One Vacancy, Full Establishment Context

Every vacancy links straight back to its funded post — team, Agenda for Change band, staff group and FTE — plus the Person Specification and Check Profile that will drive shortlisting and pre-employment checks later in the pipeline.

Applications, Interviews and Offers, One Click Away

Once a vacancy is open, smart buttons on the form give a live count of its Applications, Interviews and Offers, with a Bulk Shortlist action ready to score every received application in one pass.

My Vacancies: a Hiring Manager's Own Scope

Hiring managers see only the vacancies where they are named Hiring Manager — enforced by a record rule, not just a filter — so "My Vacancies" is always their own scope and nothing else.

Approvals Waiting on Workforce & Finance Sign-off

A vacancy can require Workforce approval before Finance approval, and Finance approval before it can open for advertising — the Approvals list shows exactly what is still waiting on a sign-off, and who submitted it.

Switch On the Public Application Form

One toggle in Settings enables the public application form and generates its access token — share that token and candidates can apply without ever needing an Odoo account.

A Public Careers Page, No Login Required

Open vacancies are advertised on a token-gated public page — candidates browse current vacancies and hit Apply straight from the listing, reusing the same secure public-form pattern as the rest of the NHS suite.

One Form Captures the Application — and Keeps Equality Data Separate

Candidate details, supporting statement, employment history and an eligibility pre-screen sit on the same form as an optional Equality & Diversity Monitoring section — captured separately and never shown to the selection panel.

An Instant Acknowledgement, With a Reference Number

On submission the candidate gets an immediate on-screen confirmation with their application reference — the same reference that tracks their application through every stage of the pipeline from the recruiter's side.

The Applications Board: Received to Interview at a Glance

Every application lands on a kanban board grouped by pipeline stage — Received, Shortlisting, Shortlisted, Interview, Offered, Hired — the recruiter's main working view of who is where.

Start Shortlisting With One Click

A received application moves into Shortlisting from its own form — the eligibility pre-screen flags for right-to-work and professional registration sit right alongside the candidate's supporting statement and employment history.

Score and Decide an Entire Shortlist at Once

The Bulk Shortlist wizard lists every candidate against the vacancy with their score and an Outcome — Shortlisted, Not Shortlisted or Hold — so a whole batch of applications can be decided from one screen instead of one at a time.

Score Against the Person Specification

Each application's Shortlisting Scores tab lists the vacancy's own person-spec criteria — GCSEs, NVQ level, and whatever else the role demands — with a Shortlist Outcome recorded right next to the running score.

Schedule the Interview Straight From the Application

Creating an interview sets the date, time and panel members without leaving the application — Mark Declined, Rescheduled, Attended or No Show move it through its own Invited / Accepted / Attended statusbar.

Every Interview, One List

The Interviews list rolls up every interview across every vacancy with its date, invite status, outcome, total score and rank — a single screen for the whole interview schedule.

Panel Scoring, Criterion by Criterion

Each panel member scores the candidate against the same person-spec criteria used at shortlisting, and Mark Attended sits right next to the scores table so outcome and evidence live together.

Every Decision, Logged and Traceable

Once an application reaches Interview, its chatter already holds a complete history — every stage change, the shortlist outcome and the acknowledgement email sent to the candidate — so nothing about the decision is undocumented.

From Appointable to a Formal Offer

Once an interview outcome is Appointable, Make Offer turns the application straight into an offer record — no re-keying the candidate, vacancy or band, and the application's stage advances automatically.

The Offer, Linked Back to the Application

The application's own Offer tab always shows its linked offer record — the pipeline stays one continuous thread from application through offer rather than two disconnected records.

An Offer Generates Its Own Check Set

Creating an offer automatically builds its Pre-Employment Checks from the vacancy's Check Profile — here six checks, none yet cleared — so nobody has to remember to raise them by hand.

Ready to Hire — if the Checks Allow It

An accepted, unconditional offer surfaces Confirm Hire and a Checks stat button together — the hard gate configured in Settings blocks confirming the hire until every required check is cleared.

The Six NHS Employment Check Standards, Per Candidate

Identity, Right to Work, Professional Registration, Employment History & References, Criminal Record (DBS) and Occupational Health — the six mandatory standards, with DBS and Occupational Health flagged Sensitive by default.

Each Check, Its Own Auditable Record

A check moves through Not Started / In Progress / Cleared / Concern / Not Required with Mark In Progress and Flag Concern actions, and its evidence detail sits in a clearly labelled "Evidence (Restricted)" section.

Confirm Hire: Gated on Every Check Cleared

The Confirm Hire wizard shows the All Checks Cleared indicator and start date up front, with an optional "Create Workforce Member in Training Module" toggle to start training compliance from day one.

Hiring Closes the Loop Into Training Compliance

Confirming the hire can create a Workforce Member straight in the Training Compliance app — already showing 100% compliance from the checks just cleared — so training requirements attach from the candidate's very first day.

Every Offer, Tracked to Hired

The Offers list shows offer type, status, proposed start date and a live Cleared / Check Count / All Checks Cleared readout for every offer in flight or already hired.

Checks in Progress, Across Every Starter

The Pre-Employment Checks menu's "Checks in Progress" view lists every check currently being worked, by candidate and check type — the authorised checks role's day-to-day worklist.

Outstanding Checks, Grouped by Starter

"Outstanding by Starter" groups every unfinished check under the candidate it belongs to — a starter with five checks still open shows as one group of five, not five scattered rows.

Every Candidate, With Their Talent-Pool Status

The Candidates kanban shows every person who has ever applied, their email and application count, with a Talent Pool badge for anyone who has consented to be kept on file.

A Data-Minimised Candidate Record

The candidate record deliberately holds only what the process needs — name, contact details, Internal Candidate flag, Talent Pool Consent and Retention Expiry — with Anonymised as a read-only marker once the retention period runs out.

The Talent Pool: Consented Candidates, Ready for Next Time

Candidates who consented to the talent pool are exempt from the automatic retention purge — the Talent Pool view is where they live on, ready to be considered for a future vacancy.

The Recruitment Dashboard: Everything on One Screen

Open vacancies, approvals awaiting sign-off, applications in flight, average time to hire, checks outstanding and flagged concerns sit in KPI tiles above the pipeline, vacancy ageing and stage-conversion funnel — the recruitment team's daily view.

Vacancy Analytics, Sliced Any Way You Like

A pivot-and-chart analysis of every vacancy by status — Draft, Open, Submitted, Closed — with the full power of Odoo's standard graph view to group, filter and measure by any field on the vacancy.

The Funnel: Where Applications Actually Go

Time-to-Hire / Funnel analytics chart every application by stage — Shortlisting, Offered, Hired and beyond — showing conversion and drop-off across the pipeline at a glance.

Equality Monitoring — Aggregate Only, Never by Name

The Equality Monitoring report pivots the segregated equality & diversity data by application reference for anonymised, aggregate reporting only — it is never joined back to a named candidate or shown to a selection panel.

The Six Check Types, Configured Once

Check Types configuration holds the six NHS Employment Check Standards with a short code, a Sensitive flag, whether it Has Level (like DBS's Standard/Enhanced tiers) and whether it's required by default.

Check Profiles, by Staff Group

Check Profiles bundle which checks apply per staff group — Nursing & Midwifery gets Enhanced DBS plus Barred List, Estates & Ancillary gets Standard DBS — so every vacancy generates the right check set automatically.

Inside a Check Profile: Which Checks, at What Level

Each profile line pairs a check type with its level — Enhanced DBS here — and a Required toggle, giving full control over exactly what a role needs checked before it opens for advertising.

Person-Specification Templates, Reused Across Vacancies

Person-Spec Templates are built once per role and attached to every vacancy that needs them — the same Band 3, Band 5 or Band 6 template drives shortlisting and interview scoring consistently, vacancy after vacancy.

Essential and Desirable Criteria, Each With Its Own Weight

Every criterion carries a category, an Essential/Desirable flag, where it's assessed — Application or Interview — and a weight, so the shortlist score is a considered sum, not a straight average.

Advertising Channels, Tracked From Advert to Hire

NHS Jobs, Trac Jobs, the Trust website, recruitment agencies and LinkedIn are configured once as advertising channels, each flagged External, ready to attach to any vacancy's advert.

Configure the Rules Once, for Every Vacancy

Settings control Workforce and Finance approval requirements, the hard gate on pre-employment checks, and the unsuccessful-applicant retention period in months — one screen governs the whole module's behaviour.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is an application's shortlist score calculated?

Each shortlisting score line is a 0-5 score against one of the vacancy's Person Specification criteria. The Shortlist Score is the sum of every line's score multiplied by that criterion's weight — e.g. (5×3)+(4×2)+(3×1) = 26 — so it grows with the number and weight of criteria rather than sitting on a fixed 0-5 scale. The same weighted approach is used again for interview scoring.

What happens if a pre-employment check comes back with a concern?

Flagging a check — say, a DBS result — as Concern pauses that candidate's progression rather than silently letting it through. With the hard gate enabled in Settings, an unconditional offer's Confirm Hire stays blocked until every required check reaches Cleared; a soft gate leaves that judgement call to the recruiter while still surfacing the concern on the offer and dashboard.

Is equality & diversity data ever visible to the selection panel?

No. Equality & diversity monitoring is collected on its own segregated model — nhs.equality.monitoring — with its own access control, kept off the application form entirely and never readable by the viewer/officer panel roles. It surfaces only in the anonymised, aggregate Equality Monitoring report, so it can never influence, or appear to influence, a selection decision.
