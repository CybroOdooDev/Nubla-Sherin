The Vacancy-to-Hire Journey

NHS Recruitment Pipeline walks every vacancy through the same controlled route, from a funded post to a hired, checked and onboarded member of staff.

1. Raise the vacancy

A vacancy is raised against a funded establishment post, with an affordability check and workforce + finance approval sign-off before it can be advertised.

2. Build the advert

Attach the job description, define essential and desirable criteria in the person specification, write the advert text and choose advertising channels with opening and closing dates.

3. Capture applications

Candidates apply through a token-gated, rate-limited public portal form (or internally), uploading a CV, with duplicate detection and equality & diversity data collected separately from the panel.

4. Shortlist

Score every candidate against the person specification, optionally anonymised, then bulk-communicate the shortlisting outcome to applicants.

5. Interview

Set up the panel, schedule interviews, score each candidate per criterion and rank outcomes to choose the preferred candidate.

6. Make the offer

Issue a conditional or unconditional offer, generate the offer letter and keep reserve candidates on hand should the preferred candidate withdraw.

7. Run the pre-employment checks

Complete the six NHS Employment Check Standards — identity, right to work, professional registration, references, DBS and occupational health — with access to sensitive data restricted and the start date gated on completion.

8. Onboard into the establishment

Confirming the hire updates the Establishment Register's in-post FTE automatically and can create a workforce-member record in the Mandatory Training module.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a vacant funded post before I can recruit?

Yes. Every vacancy is raised against a funded post from the NHS Establishment Register, so its band, cost centre and affordability are already known. The vacancy still needs workforce and finance approval before it can be advertised, keeping recruitment tied to budgeted headcount.

How is the public application form kept secure?

The public application page is token-gated to a specific open vacancy, rate-limited to deter abuse and CSRF-protected against forged submissions. Duplicate applications are detected automatically, and equality & diversity monitoring data is collected separately from the information shown to the shortlisting panel.

What are the NHS Employment Check Standards?

They are the six mandatory pre-employment checks NHS employers must complete before a new starter's first day: identity verification, right to work, professional registration, references, a Disclosure and Barring Service (DBS) check and occupational health clearance. Access to the underlying evidence is restricted, and the confirmed start date is gated until all six checks are complete.

What happens to the Establishment Register once someone is hired?

Confirming a hire automatically updates the funded post's in-post FTE in the NHS Establishment Register, closing the vacancy gap. It can also create a workforce-member record for the new starter in the NHS Mandatory Training module, so their induction and statutory training obligations are ready from day one.
