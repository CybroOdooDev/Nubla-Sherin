Key Highlights

Auto Site → Location Sync

Every Trust Site is automatically mirrored as a site-level Incident Location so reporters can select it directly without manual duplication.

Department → Unit Location Sync

Trust Departments are synced as unit-level locations nested under their parent site, keeping the full ward hierarchy available in the incident location picker.

CQC Inspection ↔ Incident Link

Patient safety incidents can be linked to formal CQC inspection visits so quality leads can cross-reference safety events against inspection findings and ratings.

Trust-Scoped Location Picker

Selecting an NHS Trust on an incident automatically filters the location dropdown to only show sites and departments registered under that trust, preventing cross-trust mismatches.

Zero Configuration Auto-Install

The bridge activates automatically when both odoo_nhs_incident_risk and odoo_nhs_trust_operations are installed. No settings to configure — all links are created instantly.

Trust Site Automatic Incident Location Mapping

When a Trust Site is created, the system automatically generates and links a corresponding Incident Location. This ensures that incident reporting locations remain synchronized with the Trust Management structure, eliminating duplicate data entry and providing consistent site selection across incidents, risks, and investigations.

Trust Department Automatic Incident Location Synchronization

When a department is created within a Trust Site, the system automatically generates and links a corresponding Incident & Risk location beneath the parent site location. This ensures incidents, risks, investigations, and actions can be accurately recorded against the correct department without maintaining duplicate location structures.

Incident Locations Automatic Trust Location Synchronization

Trust Sites and Departments created in the NHS Trust Management module are automatically synchronized as Incident Locations in the Incident & Risk Management module. This ensures a single, consistent location hierarchy is available for incident reporting, investigations, actions, and risk management across the organization.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does this bridge module do?

This bridge connects odoo_nhs_incident_risk with odoo_nhs_trust_operations. It automatically mirrors Trust Sites and Departments as Incident Locations, scopes the incident location picker to the selected trust, and links patient safety incidents to CQC Inspection records for cross-referencing safety events against inspection findings.

Do I need to configure anything after installing?

No. The bridge activates automatically when both parent modules are installed. Existing Trust Sites and Departments are synced via the post-install hook. All new sites and departments will create their linked Incident Locations automatically from that point on.

How are CQC Inspections connected to incidents?

On the incident form, the Statutory tab includes a CQC Inspections section. Quality leads can pick one or more inspections from the trust's inspection history to link them to the incident. The CQC Inspection record then shows a Linked Incidents tab and a smart button count, allowing navigation in both directions.
