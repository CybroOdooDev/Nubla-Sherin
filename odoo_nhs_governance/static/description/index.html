NHS Governance Management

Board & committee management, meeting cycles, actions, declarations of interest and the Board Assurance Framework — the corporate-governance layer of the NHS suite.

Odoo 19.0 Healthcare / NHS Board & Committee Governance

Built for the Company Secretary's office: run the committee structure and Terms of Reference, the meeting cycle (agendas, papers, minutes), the actions arising, declarations of interest, and the Board Assurance Framework (BAF) that links strategic risks to their controls and assurances — one integrated governance record instead of a pack tool plus a pile of spreadsheets.

Key Features

Committee & Board Register

Board → committee → sub-committee/group hierarchy, Terms of Reference, membership, quorum rules and meeting frequency, all with ToR-review reminders.

Meeting Cycle Management

Generate recurring meeting series, build agendas from the annual cycle of business, capture attendance with a live quoracy indicator, and assemble the full board pack.

Actions & Matters Arising

Decisions and actions from every meeting, tracked to completion with owners, due dates, overdue escalation and reporting back to the next meeting.

Declarations of Interest

A register per member — on appointment, annual refresh and at-meeting declarations — with conflict management and a curated published register output.

Board Assurance Framework

Strategic objectives → principal risks → controls → assurances (three lines of defence) → gaps → actions, RAG-scored on the same 5×5 matrix used across the suite.

Governance Dashboard & Calendar

Upcoming meetings, overdue actions, DoI refreshes due, ToR reviews due and un-reviewed BAF risks — plus the annual cycle of business across every committee.
